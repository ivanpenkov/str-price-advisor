## 2. The 4 Potential Leakage Points Above "Gross Rent"

If Kivoya is adhering strictly to the agreement, zero company income is generated above Gross Rent. However, vacation rental property management systems (PMS) such as **Streamline VRS** offer built-in mechanisms where managers can quietly capture margin *before* reporting Gross Rent:

### Leak #1: The Cleaning Fee Spread (\$550 Charged vs. \$500 Paid)
- **Live Finding**: Live pricing scans on Airbnb and VRBO show guests are charged a **\$550.00 cleaning fee**.
- **The Audit Question**: When a guest pays \$550, does Kivoya credit \$550 to the booking ledger, pay \$500 to the cleaners, and pass the remaining **\$50.00** into Gross Rent (giving you \$41.00 and them \$9.00)?
- **The Red Flag**: If Kivoya deducts the full \$550 as "Cleaning Fee", pays the vendor \$500, and retains the **\$50.00 spread** as an internal "coordination fee", they are pocketing money above Gross Rent. Over 30 bookings/year, this equals **\$1,500.00/year** of uncredited revenue.

---

### Leak #2: Ancillary Guest Fees (Damage Waivers & Admin Fees)
- **Live Finding**: On VRBO checkout for Mar 7–11, 2027, the pre-tax total showed an extra **\$111.66** beyond base rent and cleaning fee.
- **The Mechanism in Streamline VRS**: Property managers often configure mandatory guest add-ons:
  - *Accidental Rental Damage Protection (ARDP) / Waiver*: \$69.00 – \$120.00
  - *Administrative / Booking / Processing Fee*: \$30.00 – \$75.00
- **The Red Flag**: In Streamline VRS, these fees can be configured as **"Company Income"**. If a guest pays \$100 for a damage waiver, the PMS routes that \$100 directly to Kivoya's operating account. It completely bypasses your Owner Statement and never enters "Gross Rent".

---

### Leak #3: Channel Markup Arbitrage (The 14.5% Spread)
- **Live Finding**: Kivoya sets base rent on VRBO at **\$986.25/night** (+14.48% higher than the \$861.50/night direct rate).
- **The Operational Purpose**: To inflate the OTA price so the guest covers the platform's host commission.
- **The Red Flag**: If the channel takes a smaller percentage than the 14.5% markup (or if the traveler pays the service fee), does Kivoya credit your statement with the full marked-up rent, or do they settle with you based on their lower "direct seasonal rate" (\$861.50) and pocket the difference?
  - *Example (4 nights)*: If Kivoya sells on VRBO for \$3,945 base, but reports only \$3,446 on your statement, Kivoya pockets **\$499.00** off the top, turning their 18% fee into an effective **28.4% fee**.

---

### Leak #4: Undisclosed Credit Card / Merchant Processing Deductions
- Credit card processing on direct bookings and software-connected VRBO merchant accounts costs 2.5% – 3.0%.
- **The Audit Question**: Does Kivoya absorb merchant fees out of their 18% commission (as agreed: *"Kivoya pays all channel/platform fees"*), or do they quietly deduct 3% before calculating the Gross Rent line?

- `Rent` $\rightarrow$ Owner Account
- `Cleaning Fee` $\rightarrow$ Housekeeping Payable Account / Company Account
- `Damage Waiver / Admin Fee` $\rightarrow$ Company Income Account

## 5. Audit Strategy 2: Scraped Price Tracking vs. Owner App (Zero-Cooperation Audit)

What if Kivoya drags their feet, stalls, or refuses to provide internal guest folios?

### The Problem: Streamline VRS Conceals the Channel
In Streamline VRS, the default owner statement template labels all bookings identically:
* `Type: STA` (*Short Term Accommodation*)
* `Guest: Guest G`

It deliberately suppresses the channel (Airbnb, VRBO, Direct) unless the manager explicitly turns on **"Show Lead Source on Owner Statements"**.

### How to Reveal the Booking Channel:
1. **Reservation Detail in App/Portal**:
   - Tap on any reservation in the **Owner App / Web Portal**.
   - Look for the **`Confirmation Code` / `External ID`**:
     * **Airbnb**: Always a 10-character alphanumeric code starting with letters (e.g. `HM8X2P4Q1Y`).
     * **VRBO**: Always starts with `HA-` or is an 8-digit number (e.g. `HA-2048194`).
     * **Direct Booking**: Has **no external code**—only the 5-digit Streamline ID (e.g. `#16304`).
2. **Request Lead Source Activation**:
   - Ask Kivoya: *"Could you please enable the 'Lead Source / Channel' column on our owner portal so we can see which channel each booking originated from?"* (A single checkbox toggle in Streamline settings).
